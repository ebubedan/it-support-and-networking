# Biometric Attendance System Activation

## Overview

I participated in activating/enrolling staff members on a biometric attendance system used to record employee clock-in activity.

## Work carried out

- Assisted with activating the attendance system.
- Enrolled staff biometric details as part of the setup process.
- Used an enrolment list to cross-check records against an Excel spreadsheet and identify registrations that were still incomplete.

## What I learned

- Biometric attendance systems can be used to record staff attendance.
- Accurate enrolment records are important for reliable attendance management.
- Spreadsheet cross-checking can help identify missing or incomplete registrations.

## Privacy and security

Biometric information is highly sensitive.
