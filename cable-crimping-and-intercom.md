# Cable Crimping and Intercom Cabling Exposure

## Overview

I gained practical exposure to cable preparation and intercom wiring, including observing or learning about cable combinations used in a telephone/intercom installation.

## Learning points

- Cable preparation and correct termination matter for reliable connectivity.
- Cable pairs and pinouts must follow the specification for the equipment being installed.
- A cable should be tested after termination where suitable test equipment is available.

## Further learning

I plan to practise Ethernet termination using the correct T568A or T568B standard, use a cable tester, and separately study the wiring requirements for intercom systems.

## Skills

`Structured Cabling` `Cable Crimping` `Intercom Systems`

> Note: Do not assume an intercom cable follows Ethernet pinout standards. Confirm the equipment manufacturer's wiring diagram.
