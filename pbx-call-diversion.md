# PBX and Call Diversion Exposure

## Overview

I gained exposure to a phone/PBX system and the process of diverting calls.

## What I learned

- A PBX (Private Branch Exchange) manages telephone calls within an organisation.
- Call diversion forwards calls from one extension or number to another according to configured rules.
- The exact procedure depends on the PBX model and its configuration.

## Further learning

I plan to learn extension management, call routing, voicemail and basic PBX troubleshooting.
