# Siemens PLC: Technical Observation

## Overview

I had an opportunity to observe a Siemens programmable logic controller (PLC) in a technical/workplace environment.

## What I learned

- A PLC is an industrial computer used to monitor inputs and control outputs in automated processes.
- PLCs are commonly used in industrial control and automation.
- Understanding the process being controlled is important when troubleshooting automation equipment.

## Further learning

I plan to study PLC inputs and outputs, sensors, actuators, ladder logic and safe industrial control practices.
