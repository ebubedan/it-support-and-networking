# Setting Up Wireless Printing: HP DeskJet 2720

## Overview

I worked on setting up printing from a mobile phone to an HP DeskJet 2720 printer over Wi-Fi.

## Work carried out

- Identified the printer model.
- Installed the relevant HP mobile printing application.
- Worked through connecting the phone and printer to the wireless network.
- Explored the printer's wireless controls and connection process.

## What I learned

- A phone and wireless printer generally need to be connected to a compatible network for network printing.
- The correct printer application helps a mobile device discover and send documents to the printer.
- Printer setup may require checking wireless status and network connectivity.

## Further learning

I plan to practise printing from both Android and Windows, checking printer network status, and resolving common discovery and connection problems.
